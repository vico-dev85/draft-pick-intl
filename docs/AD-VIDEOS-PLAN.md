# PickNKick Ad Videos Plan — AI-Generated, Funny, Viral

## The Core Insight

Team selection for pickup soccer is **universally awkward and unfair** everywhere in the world. Being picked last, the captain choosing his friends, the 20-minute argument over who goes where — these are identical pain points in London, Sao Paulo, Lagos, and Jakarta.

Your ads don't need to explain the product. They need to **mirror the pain with precision**, then reveal that a 10-second fix exists.

## AI Tools Recommended

| Tool | Best For | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Veo 2/3** | Action shots, field scenes, fast motion | Free tier via AI Studio | 8-sec clips, best physics |
| **Hailuo MiniMax 2.3** | Group scenes, crowd dynamics, multi-person | Best cost ratio | Good for "chaotic team selection" moments |
| **Kling AI 2.0** | Wide shots, environmental | ~$32/mo | Avoid face close-ups |
| **Runway Gen-4** | Character consistency across cuts | ~$95/mo | Good for recurring "host" character |
| **CapCut** | Assembly, text overlays, transitions | Free | Final editing for all videos |

**Critical limitation**: ALL AI video tools struggle with close-up facial expressions after 2 seconds. Plan around this — use wide shots for AI, screen recordings for app demos, real phone footage for reactions.

### Hybrid Production Workflow

1. **Environment/action shots** → AI generated (wide field, crowd, celebrations)
2. **App demo moments** → Real screen recording on phone
3. **Reaction close-ups** → Film yourself or a friend on phone (3-5 sec, zero budget)
4. **Text/hooks/CTAs** → CapCut native overlays
5. **Stitch everything** → CapCut or similar editor

**Time per video**: Half a day. Cost: AI tool subscription only.

## The Video Ads (10 Concepts)

### Comedy Structures That Work Internationally

Before the concepts — these are the humor patterns we're using. All are language-independent and proven viral:

- **Recognition humor** — "That's exactly my group" (viewers tag friends)
- **Status inversion** — The confident captain gets humbled
- **Incongruity** — Treating casual kickabout with Champions League seriousness
- **Escalation** — Small problem spirals to absurd extreme, app stops the spiral
- **Khaby Lame format** — Silent, physical, "why make it hard?" energy

## CTA Best Practices

- Place at **15-20 seconds** in a 30-second video
- For 15-second videos, CTA = final 2-3 seconds
- Must appear as **on-screen text AND spoken**
- Single action only: "Try it free tonight" > "Download now"
- Time urgency works: "Use it this Thursday" > "Check it out"

**Best CTAs for PickNKick:**
- "Try it free tonight"
- "Use it this Thursday"
- "Send this to your group chat"
- "Stop being Dave" (after Ad 5)
- "Fair teams. No arguments. Free."

- Post 2x per week
- Can be slightly longer (45-60 sec) versions of TikTok content
- Link to full tutorial video (from VIDEO-CONTENT-PLAN.md) in comments

1. Film/generate for TikTok first (vertical, raw)

## Repurposing Each Ad

Every ad concept can generate 3-5 pieces of content:

1. **The full ad** (25-35 sec)
2. **The teaser** (8-15 sec, just the hook + problem, no solution — drives comments)
3. **The meme clip** (3-6 sec, just the funniest moment, no CTA — pure engagement)
4. **The behind-the-scenes** (show the AI generation process — meta content)
5. **The reaction** (film someone reacting to the ad — second wave of content)

## Quick Reference: AI Video Prompts Style Guide

For consistency across all ads, use these prompt modifiers:

**For pitch/field scenes:**
> "amateur soccer pitch, golden hour lighting, handheld camera feel, casual sportswear, 4K, cinematic but grounded, not professional stadium"

**For comedic moments:**
> "comedic timing, slight pause before reaction, natural expressions, not over-acted, documentary style"

**For dramatic parody:**
> "epic cinematic lighting, slow motion, lens flare, dramatic but absurd contrast with casual setting"

**For group shots:**
> "diverse group of 6-10 amateur soccer players, mixed ages 20-40, casual sportswear, natural interaction"

**Avoid in prompts:**
- Professional stadiums (we want park/casual pitch)
- Uniforms (casual clothes = relatable)
- Perfect weather only (overcast/muddy = authentic)
- Too-polished cinematography (handheld = authentic)
