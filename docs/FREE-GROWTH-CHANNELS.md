# Free Growth Channels — PickNKick

Beyond Reddit and press outreach, here's every free channel that can bring real users.

---

## 1. WhatsApp Groups (Highest ROI)

This is your #1 channel. Your app already spreads through WhatsApp — lean into it.

**Find pickup football WhatsApp groups:**
- Ask friends who play in other groups to share
- Search Facebook for local pickup football groups → they usually have a WhatsApp link
- Search "pickup football [city]" or "5-a-side [city]" on Facebook
- Go to local 5-a-side facilities and ask if they have player WhatsApp groups
- Post on local community Facebook groups: "anyone play regular pickup football?"

**What to send:**
```
Hey — we built a free tool for splitting teams before kickabouts.
Instead of arguing, captains do a live draft on their phones.
Takes 2 minutes, teams shared right here in the group.

Try it: https://picknkick.com

No download, no signup needed for quick draft.
```

**Scale this:** Every group you reach has 10-30 players. If they use it once and share results, every player sees the PickNKick link.

## 2. Facebook Groups

### Groups to join and post in:
- Search: "pickup football [city]", "5 a side [city]", "casual football [city]"
- "Sunday league", "midweek football", "over 30s football"
- "futsal [city]", "indoor football [city]"
- "expat football [city]" — expats playing abroad are exactly your English-speaking users

### How to post:
Don't lead with the app. Lead with the problem.

```
Question for the group — how do you pick teams before games?

We used to argue every week until someone just built a thing where
captains pick on their phones (like a fantasy draft but for real pickup).

Curious if other groups deal with this too.
```

Let people ask for the link. Then share it.

### Facebook Pages to create:
- **PickNKick** page — post tips about running pickup games, team fairness, etc.
- Share results screenshots (anonymized) — "Tuesday night draft results"
- This builds organic reach over time

## 3. Instagram / TikTok / Reels

Short video content performs extremely well for sports apps.

### Video ideas (15-30 seconds each):

**"The argument"**
- Film your group arguing about teams before a game
- Cut to phone screen: draft happening
- Cut to everyone happy, running onto pitch
- Text overlay: "Fair teams. No arguments. picknkick.com"

**"Draft day"**
- Screen record a real draft happening
- Speed it up, add dramatic music
- Show the final teams reveal
- "We do this every week"

**"Snake draft explained"**
- Whiteboard or animation explaining why snake draft is fair
- "Last pick round 1 = first pick round 2"
- "That's why nobody argues anymore"

**"POV: You're captain"**
- Phone screen recording from captain's perspective
- Your turn notification → pick a player → crowd sound → team updates
- Feels like being in a real draft

### Hashtags:
#pickupfootball #5aside #futsal #sundayleague #footballdraft #picknkick #fairteams #footballapp #casualfootball #kickabout #weeklyfooty

### Post to:
- Instagram Reels
- TikTok
- YouTube Shorts
- All three from the same video — different audiences

Each article should:
- Be 800-1500 words

**These pages bypass your HashRouter limitation** — they're real HTML files that Google can crawl and index.

## 8. 5-a-Side Facilities & Leagues

### Contact local facilities:
- Email or visit 5-a-side/futsal centers in your area
- Offer a simple pitch: "Free tool your players can use to pick teams. We don't charge anything."
- Ask if they'll put a QR code poster on the wall or mention it in their newsletter
- Some facilities run their own leagues — PickNKick could handle their drafts

### Approach:
```
Hi [Facility Name],

We made a free web app that helps pickup groups split into fair teams
before their games. It runs a live draft on everyone's phones — takes
2 minutes, no download needed.

Would you be open to mentioning it to your regular bookers? We could
provide a simple poster with a QR code for your notice board.

Completely free, no catch — we're players ourselves and built it to
solve this problem.

https://picknkick.com

Thanks,
[Name]
```

## The Viral Loop Already Built In

Remember: PickNKick already has a built-in viral mechanic.

```
User creates draft
  → Captains join (2-3 new users see the app)
  → Results shared on WhatsApp with link
  → 10-20 players in the group see the link
  → Some click → see results → growth CTA → sign up
  → They create their own drafts for their groups
  → Cycle repeats
```

Every channel above is just about getting the first person in each WhatsApp group to try it. The app does the rest.
