# Pickup Draft — Brand & Copywriting Guide

> Used by kohot.online (Hebrew) and all international versions. Replace [Brand] with your product name.

## The One Sentence

**[Brand] moves team selection from the pitch to the phone — so when you arrive, you play.**

Every word on the site should serve this idea. We don't "split teams." We give people back the best part of their week: time on the ball.

---

## The Core Insight

Hobbyist soccer groups around the world share the same ritual: 15-20 people show up to a rented pitch, stand around for 15-20 minutes arguing about teams, and lose a chunk of expensive field time before a ball is kicked.

The product doesn't just solve a logistics problem. It solves an emotional one:
- The new guy who gets picked last and feels like an outsider
- The organizer who's tired of being the one to decide and getting blamed
- The group that pays for 90 minutes and plays 65
- The competitive players who want balanced teams, not "the loud guy picks his mates"

**We're not an app. We're the reason the game starts on time.**

---

## The Emotional Position: Wingman, Not Hero

**[Brand] is never the star. The player is the star.**

Every hobbyist soccer player has a version of themselves that's a little more than "just a guy who plays on Fridays." He's the one with the killer left foot. She's the one who always shows up. He's the one the captains fight over. [Brand]'s job is to make that player feel seen — to create the stage where their game matters.

Think of it like this: Nike says "Just Do It" and puts YOU in the spotlight. They never say "Nike shoes are amazing." The shoe is the wingman. You're the athlete.

[Brand] works the same way:
- We don't say "[Brand] has an amazing draft system"
- We say "Your game deserves a real draft"
- We don't say "Our app makes fair teams"
- We say "You pick your squad. Like a real captain"

### The Wingman Rules

1. **"You" always comes before "we"** — "Your game" not "our app." "Your team" not "our feature."
2. **Elevate the player's identity** — he's not "a user," he's a captain, a pickup footballer, a weekly warrior
3. **Make the ordinary feel significant** — a Tuesday night game on a rented pitch IS a real game. Treat it that way.
4. **Never take credit** — "Teams are ready" not "[Brand] prepared your teams." The result just... happens. We're invisible.
5. **The draft is THEIR moment** — captains picking players should feel like draft night, not like using a tool

### How This Shows Up in Copy

**Landing page:**
- Not: "[Brand] — the smart team draft app"
- Yes: "Your game. Your captains. Your draft."

**Results page:**
- Not: "Draft completed successfully by [Brand]"
- Yes: "הקבוצות שלכם להערב" (Your teams for tonight)

**Waiting room:**
- Not: "[Brand] is preparing your draft"
- Yes: "הקפטנים מתכוננים. עוד רגע בוחרים" (Captains are getting ready. Picking starts soon)

**WhatsApp share:**
- Not: "I used [Brand] to make teams"
- Yes: "הקבוצות להערב מוכנות! 👇" (Tonight's teams are ready!)

**Empty dashboard:**
- Not: "You haven't used [Brand] yet"
- Yes: "המשחק הבא שלך מחכה. מתחילים?" (Your next game is waiting. Let's go?)

### The Player Archetypes We Celebrate

Every group has these characters. Our copy should make them feel recognized:

- **The Organizer** — "You run the game. Let us handle the teams." Relief, not more work.
- **The Captain** — "You know your players. Now pick your squad." Power and pride.
- **The Reliable One** — "Shows up every week, rain or shine." Belonging.
- **The New Guy** — "Getting picked by a captain beats standing around hoping." Dignity.
- **The Competitor** — "Fair teams. No excuses. Best squad wins." Respect for the game.

We never talk down to any of them. The guy who plays once a month matters as much as the one who plays three times a week.

## Brand Personality

### We Are:
- **Confident but not arrogant** — we know this works, we don't need to oversell it
- **Direct** — short sentences, no filler, say it and move on
- **Sporty-casual** — like talking to a teammate, not a brand
- **Slightly cheeky** — we can joke about the chaos we replace
- **Inclusive** — every player matters, not just the best ones

### We Are NOT:
- Corporate or formal
- Overly excited (no "AMAZING!!!" energy)
- Preachy or lecture-y
- Tech-focused (never lead with "our algorithm" or "real-time sync")
- Apologetic ("just a simple tool" — no, we're essential)

### Voice Examples

**Yes:**
> קבוצות מוכנות לפני שהגעתם. מגיעים, משחקים, נקודה.

**No:**
> האפליקציה שלנו מאפשרת לכם לבצע חלוקה הוגנת לקבוצות באמצעות מנגנון דראפט מתקדם בזמן אמת.

**Yes:**
> 5 דקות בוואטסאפ. 20 דקות יותר כדורגל.

**No:**
> חסכו זמן יקר בעזרת הפתרון הדיגיטלי המהפכני שלנו!

## Key Messaging Angles

Use these as the building blocks for all copy. Rotate them, remix them, but always come back to one of these.

### 1. Time Reclaimed
The most concrete, universal angle. Everyone understands wasted time.

- "דראפט של 5 דקות = 20 דקות יותר כדורגל הערב"
- "20 דקות נחסכות כל שבוע. על פני שנה? זה 17 משחקים שלמים"
- "אתם משלמים על 90 דקות מגרש. למה לשחק רק 65?"
- "The draft takes 5 minutes. The argument it replaces takes 20."

### 2. Teams Before the Pitch
The "how it works" angle — but framed as a lifestyle upgrade, not a feature.

- "קבוצות מוכנות לפני שהגעתם. מגיעים, משחקים, נקודה"
- "בזמן שקבוצות אחרות עדיין מתווכחות, אתם כבר משחקים"
- "הדראפט קורה בוואטסאפ. המשחק מתחיל ברגע שמגיעים"
- "No more standing around. Teams are set before anyone laces up."

### 3. Fair and Fun
The emotional angle — drafting is better than arguing, snake order is fairer than the loud guy choosing.

- "3 קפטנים, סדר הוגן, אפס ויכוחים. ככה בוחרים קבוצות"
- "לא הבן אדם הכי רועש בוחר. הדראפט בוחר"
- "סנייק דראפט — הקפטן הראשון לא מקבל יתרון. כולם שווים"
- "Every player gets picked by strategy, not by politics."

### 4. The Real Draft Experience
The aspirational angle — you're doing what the pros do, for your Tuesday game.

- "דראפט אמיתי. כמו שקבוצות אמיתיות בוחרות שחקנים"
- "הגרלת סדר, בחירה חיה, תור אחרי תור. זה לא רנדומלי — זה אמיתי"
- "Your Tuesday night game deserves a proper draft."

### 5. The Organizer's Relief
Speaking directly to the one person who always gets stuck managing the group.

- "נמאס לך להחליט קבוצות ולשמוע תלונות? תן לדראפט להחליט"
- "אתה מארגן את המשחק. אתה לא צריך גם לחלק את הקבוצות"
- "שתף לינק בקבוצה. הקפטנים בוחרים. אתה חופשי"
- "You organize the game. Let [Brand] organize the teams."

### 6. Social Proof / Community
As the product grows, use this angle to create belonging.

- "השבוע 200 קבוצות עשו דראפט עם כוחות"
- "הצטרפו לקבוצות שכבר לא מבזבזות זמן במגרש"
- "Thousands of pickup groups draft before they play."

## Tone by Context

### Landing Page
**Confident, punchy, aspirational.** This is the first impression. Lead with the strongest angle (Time Reclaimed or Teams Before the Pitch). Max 3 lines before the CTA.

Don't explain how it works on the landing page. Make them want to try it.

### Waiting Room
**Casual, reassuring, educational.** Captains are waiting — give them value. Rotate fun facts, tips, and the messaging angles as idle-state content. Keep it light.

### During the Draft
**Minimal. Functional. Urgent.** When it's your turn, don't distract with copy. "תורך לבחור!" is enough. The UX speaks here, not the words.

### Results Page
**Celebratory, shareable.** This is the moment people screenshot and send. The copy should make the result feel like an event, not a spreadsheet.

### WhatsApp Share Messages
**Ultra-short, action-oriented.** People skim WhatsApp. You get one line to hook them.

- Bad: "הנה תוצאות הדראפט שעשינו באפליקציית כוחות אונליין"
- Good: "הקבוצות להערב מוכנות! 👇"

### Error States / Empty States
**Honest, helpful, never blaming.** If something breaks, own it simply.

- "משהו השתבש. ננסה שוב?"
- "לא מצאנו את החדר הזה. בדקו את הקוד"

Never: "You entered an invalid code" — it's "we couldn't find this room."

### Onboarding / First Use
**Welcoming, zero jargon.** Assume the user has never heard the word "draft" in a sports context. Show, don't explain.

## Page-by-Page Copy Guidelines

### Landing Page (/)

**Hero section — one message, one CTA:**
- Headline: strongest angle, 5-8 words max
- Sub-headline: one supporting sentence
- CTA: "התחילו דראפט" (Start a Draft) — not "Sign Up" or "Learn More"
- Secondary CTA: "הצטרפו לדראפט" (Join a Draft) with room code input

**Do NOT put on the landing page:**
- Feature lists
- "How it works" steps (save for scroll-down or separate page)
- Technical language
- Multiple competing CTAs

**Examples:**

Option A (Time angle):
> **מגיעים למגרש ומתחילים לשחק**
> קבוצות נבחרות בדראפט חי לפני המשחק. בלי ויכוחים, בלי בזבוז זמן.

Option B (Fair angle):
> **דראפט אמיתי למשחק שלכם**
> קפטנים בוחרים שחקנים בתור. הוגן, מהיר, דרך וואטסאפ.

Option C (Direct):
> **הקבוצות להערב? מוכנות**
> 5 דקות דראפט בוואטסאפ. 20 דקות יותר כדורגל.

### Dashboard (/dashboard)

Minimal copy. This is a workspace, not a marketing page. Use action-oriented labels:
- "דראפט חדש" not "צור דראפט חדש"
- "השחקנים שלי" not "ניהול רשימת שחקנים"
- Empty state: "עוד לא עשית דראפט. מתחילים?" with CTA

### Quick Draft Flow (/quick-draft)

Guide without lecturing. Each step should have a short instruction:
- Step 1: "איך קוראים למשחק?" (placeholder, not label)
- Step 2: "מי מגיע הערב?" with "הוסיפו שחקנים" as the action
- Step 3: "בחרו 3 קפטנים" — keep it that simple

### Waiting Room (/room/:code)

**Rotating messages while waiting (every 5-6 seconds):**

Pool of messages to rotate through:

*Value props:*
- "בזמן שאתם כאן, קבוצות אחרות עדיין מתווכחות במגרש"
- "דראפט של 5 דקות = 20 דקות יותר כדורגל הערב"
- "3 קפטנים, סדר הוגן, אפס ויכוחים"
- "קבוצות מוכנות לפני שהגעתם. מגיעים, משחקים, נקודה"
- "כל שבוע 20 דקות נחסכות. על השנה? זה עוד 17 משחקים"

*Tips:*
- "טיפ: שתפו את הלינק בקבוצת הוואטסאפ של המשחק"
- "טיפ: הקפטן הראשון בסנייק לא מקבל יתרון — הסדר מתהפך כל סיבוב"
- "טיפ: אפשר לעשות דראפט גם לכדורסל, כדורעף, או כל משחק קבוצתי"

*Fun/cheeky:*
- "בינתיים תחשבו מי אתם בוחרים ראשון..."
- "הגרלת הסדר עוד רגע. מי יבחר ראשון?"
- "דראפט > להתווכח 15 דקות ולקבל קבוצות לא הוגנות"

### Draft Board (/draft/:code)

**Absolute minimum copy.** The UI does the talking.

- Turn indicator: "תורך לבחור!" / "[שם] בוחר..."
- Confirm modal: "בוחרים את [שם]?" with "כן" / "לא"
- Pool header: "שחקנים זמינים (X)"
- That's it. No explanations, no tips, no marketing.

### Results Page (/results/:code)

**Header area:**
- Club/draft name + date
- "הקבוצות להערב" or "תוצאות הדראפט"

**After teams display:**
- Share CTA: "שתפו בוואטסאפ" (primary, prominent)
- Small text under share: "שלחו לקבוצה — כולם ידעו עם מי הם משחקים"

**Future — gear recommendations section:**
- Header: "מוכנים למשחק?" or "ציוד למשחק הערב"
- Keep it one line per item, feels like a recommendation from a friend
- "חסרים סינרים? חבילת 3 צבעים →"

### WhatsApp Share Templates

**Draft invite (to captains):**
> ⚽ דראפט למשחק הערב!
> היכנסו ובחרו שחקנים:
> [link]

**Results share (to group):**
> ⚽ הקבוצות להערב מוכנות!
> [link]

**Keep it to 2 lines + link.** WhatsApp previews truncate after ~3 lines. The link preview (OG tags) does the rest.

### Auth / Login Page

**Don't oversell. They're already here to sign up.**
- "התחברות" as the page title
- "התחבר עם Google" as primary (one tap, zero friction)
- Email/password as secondary, collapsed
- Footer: "אין לך חשבון? ההרשמה חינמית ולוקחת 5 שניות"

### Error / 404 / Expired Draft

**Honest and helpful:**
- Expired draft: "הדראפט הזה הסתיים. רוצים להתחיל חדש?"
- Room not found: "לא מצאנו את החדר. הקוד נכון?"
- Generic error: "משהו לא עבד. ננסה שוב?"

Always offer a next step. Never leave them at a dead end.

## Words We Use / Don't Use

### Our Vocabulary
| Use | Don't Use |
|-----|-----------|
| דראפט | חלוקה / הגרלה |
| קפטן | ראש קבוצה / מנהיג |
| בוחרים | ממיינים / מחלקים |
| משחק | אירוע / פעילות |
| קבוצה | צוות |
| שחקנים | משתתפים |
| הוגן | אובייקטיבי / שוויוני |
| מגרש | מתחם / אולם |

### English Vocabulary (for international version)
| Use | Don't Use |
|-----|-----------|
| Draft | Sort / Divide / Randomize |
| Captain | Team leader / Picker |
| Pick | Select / Choose / Assign |
| Pickup game | Casual game / Friendly |
| Pitch | Field / Venue |
| Fair | Balanced / Optimal / Algorithmic |

### Words That Kill Our Vibe
- "אלגוריתם" — we're not a tech company, we're a sports experience
- "אופטימלי" — too corporate
- "פלטפורמה" — we're an app, not a platform
- "משתמשים" — they're players, not users
- "פיצ'ר" — describe what it does, not what it is
- "Revolutionary" / "מהפכני" — let them decide that

## Copy Length Rules

| Context | Max Length |
|---------|-----------|
| Landing headline | 8 words |
| Landing sub-headline | 15 words |
| Button label | 3 words |
| Toast/notification | 10 words |
| WhatsApp share | 2 lines + link |
| Waiting room message | 15 words |
| Error message | 10 words + one action |
| Feature description | 1 sentence |

**If you're writing more than 2 sentences anywhere in the app UI, you're writing too much.**

## The Emotional Journey

Map copy tone to where the player is emotionally:

```
Landing     → Curious, skeptical      → Be confident, prove value fast
Sign up     → Willing but impatient   → Zero friction, no lengthy forms
Create game → Excited, organizing     → Guide clearly, keep momentum
Waiting     → Bored, idle             → Entertain, educate, build hype
Raffle      → Anticipation            → Let the animation speak
Drafting    → Focused, competitive    → Get out of the way
Results     → Satisfied, social       → Celebrate, make sharing easy
Share       → Proud, showing off      → Make the message look premium
```

## Writing for WhatsApp Context

Most of the product's distribution happens inside WhatsApp. Every piece of outbound text should be written as if it's being read:
- On a small screen
- In a chat with 50 unread messages above it
- By someone who will spend 2 seconds deciding to tap or scroll past

**Rules for WhatsApp-bound copy:**
1. Lead with an emoji — it stops the scroll
2. First line = the hook (what's in it for them)
3. Link on its own line — easier to tap
4. No more than 3 lines before the link
5. No formal language — write like you'd text a friend

## International Adaptation Notes

When translating to English/Spanish/etc:
- Don't translate literally — adapt the angle to local sports culture
- UK/Australia: "pitch" not "field"
- US: "pickup game" — this term is universal there
- Latin America: futbolito / pichanga / cascarita — use local slang
- The core message is universal but the flavor is local
- "Draft" as a concept is stronger in US (fantasy sports culture) — lean into it there
- In Europe, "picking teams" is more relatable than "draft"

## Measuring Copy Effectiveness

Once analytics are in place, track:
- **Landing → CTA click rate** — is the headline working?
- **WhatsApp share rate** on results page — is the share CTA compelling?
- **Draft completion rate** — are instructions clear enough?
- **Return rate** — are players coming back?

If any of these drop, the copy is the first thing to revisit.
