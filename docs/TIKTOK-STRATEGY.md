## How the Algorithm Works in 2026

Understanding this determines everything about your content.

### What Gets Pushed

| Signal | Weight | What It Means for You |
|--------|--------|----------------------|
| **Watch time / completion rate** | ~40-50% | Keep videos short enough to finish. 70%+ completion rate needed to go viral (up from 50% in 2024). |
| **Shares and saves** | Higher than likes | Make content people want to send to their football group chat. Shares > likes now. |
| **First 30 minutes engagement** | Critical | Early comments, shares, and rewatches in the first 30 min make or break reach. |
| **Rewatches** | Very high | Loops, reveals, and "wait for it" moments trigger rewatches. |
| **Comments** | High | Ask questions, create debates, use controversial takes to trigger comments. |

### How Distribution Works

1. Your video shows to a **small test audience** (followers first)
2. If they engage (watch, share, comment), it goes to a **wider pool**
3. If that pool engages, it hits the **For You Page** (FYP) for your niche
4. If FYP engagement is strong, it goes **broad** — potentially millions

**Key takeaway:** Your first followers matter. Build a small engaged base of football players before expecting virality.

### The Hook Rule

**63% of highest-CTR videos** deliver their key message in the first 3 seconds. The algorithm measures if people keep watching — if they scroll past in 2 seconds, you're dead.

Every video must open with a hook:
- A bold text overlay ("This is why your team always loses")
- A question ("How does your group pick teams?")
- Visual action (phone screen, people arguing, ball being kicked)
- A payoff preview ("Watch what happens when captains draft live")

## Video Format Spec

### Optimal Length

| Length | Best For | Completion Rate |
|--------|----------|-----------------|
| **15-30 seconds** | Viral reach, highest engagement | Highest — people watch the whole thing |
| **21-34 seconds** | Sweet spot for storytelling + engagement | High |
| **7-15 seconds** | Quick hooks, memes, reactions | Very high completion but less depth |
| **60+ seconds** | Tutorials, full draft recordings | Lower completion but higher total watch time |

**Default to 15-25 seconds.** Only go longer when the content demands it (full draft walkthrough, tutorial).

### Technical Specs

- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 (vertical, full screen)
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 minimum
- **Format:** MP4
- **No watermarks** — never post with another platform's watermark. Always export clean from editor.
- **Captions/subtitles:** Always add text overlays — many people watch without sound
- **Music:** Use TikTok's trending sounds when relevant (check Creative Center for trending audio)

## Content Pillars — The 4 Types of Videos

### Pillar 1: "The Problem" (Relatable Moments)

**The most important pillar.** These are the videos that go viral because every pickup player has lived this exact moment.

| Video Idea | Hook (First 3 Sec) | Length |
|------------|-------------------|--------|
| "The argument" | Text: "Every. Single. Week." + people arguing | 15-20s |
| "The guy who always picks his mates" | POV: captain picking, everyone groaning | 15-20s |
| "When you get picked last" | Slow zoom on face, dramatic music | 10-15s |
| "The 'fair' team picker" | Text: "When one person picks both teams 'fairly'" | 15-20s |
| "Waiting 15 minutes to start because of teams" | Time-lapse of people standing around | 15-20s |
| "When the teams are 'random' but one team has all the good players" | Cut between stacked team and weak team | 15-20s |
| "The group chat the day before a game" | Screen recording of WhatsApp chaos | 15-20s |

**Why these work:** Relatable = shareable. People tag their football group. Shares are the #1 algorithm signal.

**CTA:** Don't hard-sell. End with text overlay: "There's a better way" or "Link in bio" or just the PickNKick logo.

### Pillar 2: "The Solution" (Product Demos)

Show the app in action. Screen recordings + real reactions.

| Video Idea | Hook (First 3 Sec) | Length |
|------------|-------------------|--------|
| "Draft day" | Phone screen: draft starting, dramatic music | 20-30s |
| "POV: You're captain" | Your turn notification → pick player → crowd sound | 20-25s |
| "2 minutes. Fair teams. No arguments." | Split screen: arguing (left) vs draft (right) | 15-20s |
| "The team reveal" | Final teams appearing on screen with reactions | 15-20s |
| "How we pick teams now" | Before (arguing) → After (calm draft on phones) | 20-25s |
| "Quick Draft in 60 seconds" | Speed-up tutorial: type names → draft → done | 30-45s |

**Key:** Show real phones, real people, real reactions. Not polished ads. TikTok punishes content that looks like an ad.

### Pillar 3: "Football Culture" (Community Content)

Content that football players share because it's about them, not about your app. This builds your following so product videos get distribution.

| Video Idea | Hook (First 3 Sec) | Length |
|------------|-------------------|--------|
| "Ranking ways to pick teams" | Tier list: worst to best | 20-30s |
| "Types of players in every pickup group" | The show-off, the late guy, the complainer... | 20-30s |
| "Unwritten rules of 5-a-side" | Text list with dramatic music | 15-25s |
| "Things pickup players say" | Compilation: "I was open!", "That's a foul!" | 15-20s |
| "The pickup football starter pack" | Meme format with images | 10-15s |
| "What position do you play in pickup?" | Comment bait — everyone has an opinion | 10-15s |
| "Your group has that one guy who..." | Relatable series, new "guy" each week | 15-20s |

**Why these matter:** These get 10x the reach of product videos. They build your audience of football players. When you drop a product video between these, it reaches the right people.

### Pillar 4: "Behind the Build" (Indie/Dev Content)

TikTok's dev/builder community is huge and supportive. These cross-pollinate with r/SideProject and Indie Hackers audiences.

| Video Idea | Hook (First 3 Sec) | Length |
|------------|-------------------|--------|
| "I built an app for my football group" | Screen recording of app + text overlay | 20-30s |
| "Day 1 vs Day 100 of building a football app" | Side-by-side: ugly prototype vs polished product | 15-20s |
| "The hardest bug I had to fix" | Code on screen → explain in plain English | 20-30s |
| "How I made the draft order cryptographically fair" | Quick explainer with visuals | 20-30s |
| "When your friends actually use the thing you built" | Reaction video / screen recording | 15-20s |

## Hashtag Strategy

### The Rules (2026)

- **3-5 hashtags per video.** More than 5 confuses the algorithm and looks spammy.
- **Put hashtags in the caption, not comments.** Caption hashtags = 100% algorithm weight. Comment hashtags = only 30-40%.
- **Mix:** 1-2 trending + 2-3 niche + 0-1 branded.
- **Niche hashtags deliver 60-70% higher engagement** than broad ones.
- TikTok now "listens" to your video — make sure spoken words and text overlays match your hashtags.

## Cross-Posting to Reels + Shorts

One video, three platforms. But do it right.

### The Rules

1. **Never cross-post with watermarks.** Export the clean original from your editor. Instagram suppresses TikTok watermarks. YouTube suppresses Instagram watermarks.
2. **Don't copy-paste the same caption.** Each platform has different caption culture:
   - **TikTok:** Short (under 150 chars), conversational, 3-5 hashtags
   - **Instagram Reels:** Slightly longer, can include a CTA, 5-10 hashtags
   - **YouTube Shorts:** Keyword-rich title (YouTube is a search engine), 3-5 hashtags
3. **Stagger posting times.** Don't post everywhere simultaneously — audience overlap causes lower engagement on the 2nd and 3rd platform.
   - Post to **TikTok first** (your primary platform)
   - **Instagram Reels** 2-4 hours later
   - **YouTube Shorts** 4-8 hours later

### Platform-Specific Adjustments

| Platform | Caption Style | Hashtags | Extra |
|----------|-------------|----------|-------|
| TikTok | Short, casual, question or hook | 3-5 niche | Use trending sounds |
| Instagram Reels | Slightly polished, CTA "link in bio" | 5-10 mixed | "Send to your football group" (DM shares boost Reels) |
| YouTube Shorts | Keyword-rich, descriptive title | 3-5 in description | Title = SEO ("How to pick fair teams for pickup soccer") |

## Production: How to Film With Zero Budget

You don't need a camera crew. You need a phone and your weekly football game.

### Equipment

- Your phone (any modern smartphone)
- A cheap phone tripod/mount ($10-15)
- Natural lighting (outdoor pitch is perfect)
- Optional: clip-on mic for voice-over ($15)

### What to Film at Your Weekly Game

Every game night is content. Film these:

| Moment | How to Film | Becomes |
|--------|------------|---------|
| Pre-game chaos (arguing about teams) | Film from the side, capture the banter | "The problem" video |
| The draft happening on phones | Screen record your phone + film others looking at theirs | Product demo |
| Captain picking, reactions | Film the group during draft | Reaction content |
| Team reveal moment | Film reactions when teams are announced | "The reveal" video |
| The actual game (goals, skills, fails) | Quick clips, 5-10 seconds each | Football culture content |
| Post-game banter | "Those teams were actually fair for once" | Testimonial / culture |

**Batch film:** Film 5-10 raw clips at one game night. Edit them into 3-5 videos throughout the week. One game night = one week of content.

### Editing

- **CapCut** (free, TikTok's own editor) — best for TikTok-native editing
- Add text overlays (always — many watch on mute)
- Add trending sounds or dramatic music
- Cut fast — no dead air, no long pauses
- Zoom in on key moments (phone screen, facial reactions)

### Screen Recording the App

- Use your phone's built-in screen recorder
- Show the draft flow: create → captains join → pick → reveal
- Speed up the boring parts (entering names)
- Add text explaining what's happening
- Show the WhatsApp share at the end

## Growth Tactics (Beyond Just Posting)

### 1. Comment on Football TikToks

Spend 10 minutes/day commenting on popular football content. Not "check out my app" — genuine, funny comments about the video. People check profiles of funny commenters. Your profile links to PickNKick.

### 2. Reply to Comments With Video

When someone comments something interesting on your video, reply with a new video. TikTok promotes comment-reply videos because they drive engagement chains.

Example: Someone comments "LOL my group would never agree on captains" → Reply video showing the random captain raffle feature.

### 3. Duet/Stitch Football Content

Find popular football TikToks about teams, fairness, or pickup games. Stitch or duet them with your take. This piggybacks on their audience.

Example: Stitch a video of someone complaining about unfair teams → "Here's what we do instead" → show the draft.

### 4. Use TikTok's "Promote" Feature Strategically

TikTok lets you boost posts for small amounts ($5-20). Only boost videos that are **already performing organically**. If a video gets good engagement in the first hour, a small boost can push it over the edge into viral territory.

**Never boost a video that's flopping.** Paid can amplify momentum, not create it.

### 5. Engage in the First 30 Minutes

The first 30 minutes after posting are critical. Be ready to:
- Reply to every comment instantly
- Pin the best comment
- Ask a follow-up question in the comments yourself ("What does your group do?")

This signals to the algorithm that your video is generating conversation.

## Tracking Success

### Metrics That Matter

| Metric | Target (Month 1) | Why |
|--------|------------------|-----|
| **Completion rate** | 70%+ | Algorithm threshold for wider distribution |
| **Shares per video** | 10+ | Shares > likes for reach |
| **Profile visits** | 50+/week | People checking you out after seeing content |
| **Link clicks (bio)** | 20+/week | Actual conversion to picknkick.com |
| **Follower growth** | 100-500/month | Building your base of football players |
| **Best performing content type** | Track | Double down on what works |

### What to Optimize

After 2 weeks of posting, review:
1. **Which pillar gets the most shares?** → Make more of that
2. **What hook format works?** → Text overlay vs voice vs action
3. **Which hashtags drive the most views?** → Refine your sets
4. **What time slots get the most engagement?** → Adjust schedule
5. **What's your average completion rate?** → If under 70%, make videos shorter
