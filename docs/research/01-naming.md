# Research Prompt: App Name

## Your Task

Determine the best name for a soccer/football team draft app. Evaluate the current name "Draft Pick" and provide alternatives. This research should end with a clear recommendation.

---

## App Context

**What it does:** A live snake draft app for pickup soccer. Organizers create a club, add their player roster, then on game day: select tonight's players, pick captains, run a live draft where captains pick players in snake order from their phones, share final teams via WhatsApp, and optionally track the game (timer, goals, standings).

**Core value prop:** "Fair teams. No arguments."

**Target users:** Casual pickup soccer groups — friends who play weekly, organize via WhatsApp, 6-20 players. International audience (English default, expanding to Spanish, French, German, Italian, Dutch).

**Distribution:** Primarily through WhatsApp shares. Every time someone shares draft results, the link acts as an ad. Secondary: App Store search, social media.

**Business model:** Free forever (growth-first, monetize later).

**Competitor names in the space:**
- Team pickers: "Team Picker", "AppSorteos" (generic, unmemorable)
- Pickup game finders: "Footy Addicts", "Playfinder", "GoodRec", "Pickup"
- Team management: "TeamSnap", "Spond", "SportEasy", "Mingle Sport"
- Fantasy drafts: "Sleeper", "Sorare"
- Soccer apps: "FotMob", "OneFootball"

---

## Current Name: "Draft Pick"

**What works:**
- Immediately communicates function (draft + pick)
- 10 characters (under 11-char mobile truncation limit)
- Two strong consonant-heavy words (D, R, F, T + P, K)
- 2 words, 2 syllables each — punchy rhythm
- Works phonetically in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Dutch

**What concerns us:**
- "Draft pick" is generic sports terminology (every NFL/NBA fan knows it)
- May be hard to trademark as it's descriptive
- Could be confused with beer drafts in some cultures
- Domain draftpick.com is certainly taken
- Multiple apps may already use this name in app stores

---

## Naming Constraints

1. **Under 11 characters** — longer names get truncated on phone home screens
2. **1-3 syllables** with emphasis on the first syllable — this is the rhythm pattern that sticks
3. **Pronounceable in 6 languages** — EN, ES, FR, DE, IT, NL. Must not have embarrassing meanings in any of these
4. **Strong consonants** (T, R, S, P, D, K) — these punch more and create memorability
5. **Not locked to soccer** — the draft mechanic works for basketball, volleyball, etc. Name shouldn't prevent expansion
6. **Domain available** — .com, .app, or .io
7. **App Store clear** — no major competing app with the same name
8. **WhatsApp-friendly** — when someone sees "Sent via [AppName]" in a WhatsApp message, it should be instantly clear this is about team picking

---

## Research Questions

1. **Domain and trademark search:** Is draftpick.com available? What about variations (.app, .io)? Are there existing apps named "Draft Pick" in Apple App Store and Google Play?

2. **Linguistic check:** Does "Draft Pick" translate well or cause confusion in Spanish, French, German, Italian, Dutch? "Draft" in German means "wire" — does that matter?

3. **Name directions to explore:**

   | Direction | Examples | Strengths | Risks |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Keep current | Draft Pick | Clear, established | Generic, trademark risk |
   | Compound action | KickDraft, PickDay, DraftNight | Descriptive + unique | May sound forced |
   | Single action word | Lineup, Whistle, Shuffle | Simple, brandable | May not communicate "draft" |
   | Soccer metaphor | Pitch, Formation, Kickoff | Evocative | May limit to soccer |
   | Coined/portmanteau | Draftly, Pickster, Squadra | Unique, trademarkable | Zero instant meaning |
   | Abstract | Something like Sleeper, Strava | Max brand flexibility | Requires brand-building effort |

4. **The WhatsApp test:** Imagine this footer in a WhatsApp message:
   ```
   ⚡ Fair teams. No arguments. — [AppName]
   ```
   Which name makes someone curious enough to click?

5. **The home screen test:** A 10-character label under an app icon. Which name looks best truncated?

---

## Deliverable

Provide:
1. **Evaluation of "Draft Pick"** — should we keep it? Score 1-10 on: memorability, uniqueness, trademark viability, multi-language safety, domain availability
2. **Top 5 alternative names** — each with domain availability check, linguistic notes, and a score on the same criteria
3. **Final recommendation** — one name with full rationale
4. **Tagline compatibility** — how does the recommended name pair with "Fair teams. No arguments."?
